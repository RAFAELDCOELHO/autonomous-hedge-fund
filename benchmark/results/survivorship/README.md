# P1.9 Survivorship bracket: distressed vs liquid B3 names

Generated by `make survivorship` (`scripts/survivorship_distress.py`). Offline:
committed Close fixtures only, no download, no LLM.

## Why

The paper tables use five liquid, continuously traded names (PETR4, VALE3,
ITUB4, BBDC4, ^BVSP). Lesmond (1999) puts the upward distortion of liquid-only
universes at roughly 1-3pp per year for 1990s emerging-market data; that
estimate is cross-decade and cross-country. This artifact brackets the effect
empirically on B3 2019-2022 by running the same classical agents on names that
went through distress inside the study window.

## Universe

| Universe | Tickers | Note |
|---|---|---|
| liquid | PETR4, VALE3, ITUB4, BBDC4, ^BVSP | the paper-five |
| distressed | OIBR3, MGLU3, AMER3 | see GOLL4 note |

**GOLL4 is not included.** Yahoo Finance raises `YFTzMissingError` for
`GOLL4.SA` (delisted, no timezone metadata), so no prices could be fetched and
none were invented. AMER3 (Americanas, accounting-fraud collapse in January
2023 with the tail visible at the end of the fixture) is the third distressed
name instead. Fixture range for all three: 2018-01-02 to 2023-01-13, same
vintage layout as the paper-five (`Date,Close`, auto-adjusted).

## Method

Identical to the paper baselines (`scripts/regime_lib.py`): long-only, binary
all-in/all-cash, frictionless, decisions at the close, warm indicators, no
look-ahead. Agents: buy_and_hold, macd (12,26,9), sma_crossover (50/200),
momentum (60), random as the N=100-seed mean (seeds 0-99). Four hand regimes:
bull_2019, crisis_2020, recovery_2021, hi_rates_2022.

## Files

- `per_cell.csv`: `universe, agent, regime, ticker, n_days, total_return_pct`
  (5 agents x 4 regimes x 8 tickers = 160 rows). CR in percent, 4 dp.
- `summary.csv`: `agent, regime, liquid_mean_cr_pct, distressed_mean_cr_pct, gap_pp`
  (20 rows). `gap_pp = liquid - distressed`, a positive gap means the liquid
  universe flatters that agent in that regime.

## Sensitivity vs Lesmond 1-3pp

Compare `gap_pp` per regime against the 1-3pp/yr Lesmond band. Regimes are
sub-annual (crisis_2020 is 4 months, recovery_2021 6 months), so scale the band
accordingly before reading a cell as inside or outside it. Three distressed
names is a bracket, not an estimate: it shows the sign and order of magnitude
the liquid-only choice can move the classical baselines by, on this market and
window.

## Caveats

- Distressed names have extreme drawdowns and split/reverse-split histories
  on Yahoo (OIBR3, AMER3); auto-adjusted Closes carry the provider's split
  handling, see the data-source limitation in `docs/brazilbench.tex`.
- Three names, four windows: descriptive, no significance test.
- Frictionless execution understates the real cost of trading illiquid names,
  so the bracket is conservative on the cost side.
